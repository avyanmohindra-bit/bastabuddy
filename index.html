THE PROBLEM

A small bag. Two big problems.

Students carry books, notebooks, stationery, lunch boxes and water bottles every day. But keeping a school bag organized and at a reasonable weight is not always easy.

01

Overweight School Bags

Carrying excessive weight can cause unnecessary strain on students' backs and shoulders. Students may not realize how heavy their bags have become.

02

Forgotten Essentials

Notebooks, pencil boxes, water bottles and ID cards can easily be forgotten at home, disrupting the school day and causing inconvenience.

OUR SOLUTION

Introducing a smarter school bag.

BastaBuddy combines simple electronics and intelligent checking to make school mornings more organized.

01

Weight Monitoring

A load cell measures the total bag weight and the system provides alerts when the chosen limit is crossed.

02

Item Detection

RFID tags or suitable item sensors help identify whether required school essentials have been packed.

03

Instant Alerts

An OLED display, LEDs and a buzzer communicate the bag's weight and missing-item warnings.

INSIDE BASTABUDDY

Meet the technology.

Every component has a specific job. Select a component to understand how it contributes to the prototype.

Arduino UNO R4 WiFi — The Brain

The Arduino UNO R4 WiFi processes information from the sensors, calculates the bag weight, checks item detection data and controls the display and alerts. Its Wi-Fi and Bluetooth capabilities can support future wireless features.

THE WORKING PRINCIPLE

How does BastaBuddy work?

A simple process connects the sensors, controller and alerts to create a smarter school bag.

01

Weight is detected

The load cell senses the bag's weight. The HX711 amplifies its signal so the Arduino can read it.

02

Items are identified

RFID tags or other suitable sensors help identify which required items are present.

03

Arduino processes the information

The Arduino UNO R4 WiFi compares the measured weight with the selected limit and checks the required-item list.

04

Alerts are generated

The display shows information while LEDs and the buzzer can warn about excessive weight or missing items.

05

Ready for school!

Students can review their bag's weight and checklist before leaving home.
